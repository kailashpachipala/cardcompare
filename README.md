# CardCompare — Find the right credit card for how YOU spend

A beginner-friendly credit card advisor for India. No CIBIL score, no sign-up, no personal data — just tell the app how you spend, and it gives you **one clear answer**.

## What it does

- **16 biggest banks** — 7 Government (SBI Card, BOBCARD, PNB, Canara, Union Bank, Bank of India, Central Bank) + 9 Private (HDFC, ICICI, Axis, Kotak, IDFC FIRST, RBL, YES Bank, IndusInd, Karur Vysya)
- **425+ cards** in the full official catalogs — every bank opens its complete card list, not just one or two
- **Personal savings engine** — enter monthly spends (shopping, food, fuel, travel, everyday) and every scored card is ranked by *your* net saving: rewards − fee after waiver − interest cost
- **Own-bank first logic** — verdicts of **KEEP** your present card / **UPGRADE** inside your bank / **SHIFT** to a better one, with the reason in plain language
- **Best for UPI** — RuPay cards carry a "UPI Ready" badge; a dedicated UPI pick shows what your UPI spends could earn (debit-card UPI earns ₹0)
- **Fee tiers for every salary** — cards grouped as **₹0 Free · Light (₹1–500) · Premium (₹501–1,500) · Super Premium (₹1,500+)**, with badges and filters
- **🎓 Student Favourite** — the slice UPI Credit Card (slice Small Finance Bank): ₹0 fee, RuPay, Pay-in-3 at no extra cost, shown as a clearly labelled spotlight
- **Beginner-safe guidance** — real-life examples built from your own spends, a credit-limit guide, and the 50-day interest-free rule explained simply
- **Official links only** — every Apply button goes to the bank's own website. Never a third-party or agent link

## How to use

1. Open the app (GitHub Pages link above, or just open `index.html` in any browser)
2. Pick a spending profile or enter your own spends
3. Choose your bank and your present card (or "I don't have a card")
4. Get one clear answer — with the saving in big numbers

## Tech

Single self-contained `index.html` (HTML + CSS + JavaScript, no build step, no backend, no tracking). Works offline once loaded.

## Data & honesty

- Card fees, rates and catalogs were collected from the banks' **official websites** (Nov 2026) — always confirm on the bank's site before applying
- Catalog-only cards never show invented savings or benefits; savings figures come only from the scoring engine for the 23 fully-modelled cards
- Estimates only — this is an educational comparison tool, not financial advice
